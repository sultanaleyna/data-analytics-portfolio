# Amazon Books Inventory Analysis 📚

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Amazon Books inventory data using Google Sheets to evaluate stock availability, identify product categories with higher shortage rates, and provide actionable recommendations for inventory management and procurement planning.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Calculate the overall inventory shortage rate.
- Analyze stock availability across product categories.
- Identify categories with the highest shortage rates.
- Highlight inventory risks and potential replenishment needs.
- Provide data-driven recommendations for inventory and procurement teams.

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills

- **Google Sheets**
- Data Analysis
- Pivot Tables
- Spreadsheet Formulas
- Inventory KPI Analysis
- Data Visualization
- Business Insights & Recommendations

## 📊 Key Findings

| Category | Shortage Rate |
|---|---:|
| Law | 1.85% |
| Medical | 1.48% |
| Tech | 1.41% |
| Fiction | 1.34% |
| Self-Help | 1.28% |

- **Overall shortage rate:** 1.37%
- **Highest-risk category:** Law, with a shortage rate of 1.85%.
- Medical and Tech are the next categories requiring attention.

## 💡 Recommendations

1. Prioritize inventory monitoring and replenishment for high-risk categories.
2. Identify individual products that require urgent restocking.
3. Compare current inventory levels with forecast demand.
4. Review supplier availability and delivery lead times.
5. Monitor shortage rates regularly to support better inventory decisions.

## 📦 Project Deliverables

- Inventory analysis spreadsheet
- Category-level shortage analysis
- Stock availability insights
- Inventory risk assessment
- Procurement recommendations

## 📈 Business Impact

This analysis supports data-driven inventory decisions by highlighting categories with higher shortage rates and helping the inventory and procurement teams prioritize replenishment activities.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

The findings reflect the analyzed dataset and its inventory snapshot. Shortage rates and replenishment priorities may change as inventory levels and forecasts are updated.
